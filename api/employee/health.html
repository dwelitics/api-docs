What it does

The same build-identity payload as app/health, for the staff app. git_sha, app_version, human_version and build_time are stamped at build time; env and vercel_deployment_id come from the Vercel runtime; ts is Date.now(). Cache-Control: no-store, dynamic = 'force-dynamic'.

production-promote.yml reads git_sha from this route on the public domain to confirm the commit that triggered the deployment event is the commit now answering, before it moves the alias.

What it does not do

  • It carries no question_cache field. apps/app's health route does; this one does not, because apps/employee reads no Metabase question. Two apps, two payload shapes — a caller that parses one must not assume the other.
  • No database, no auth, no error branch. One path, always 200.

Gotchas

  • **This route is on the EMPLOYEE host, and the employee host is a separate identity boundary.** A session for app.dwelitics.com is not a session here and never becomes one (INV-emp-boundary-1). Nothing in this route reads a session at all, but the neighbouring routes under docs/api/employee/ do, and they enforce that boundary server-side.
  • git_sha reads local on an unstamped local build, same as the app host.
